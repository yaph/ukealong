Three ways to quiz yourself

Name that Chord

A chord diagram is shown. You pick the correct name from four options.

Find that Chord

A chord name is shown. You pick the correct diagram from four options.

Ear Training

A chord is played. You pick the correct diagram from four options. Tapping an answer plays that chord and moves to the next round.

Built for how practice actually works

🎯

Four chord pools

Start with the Starter set of beginner chords and work up through Standard, Extended, and Full at your own pace.

📖

Chord reference

Browse all chords in a searchable visual library, grouped by learning tier. Includes multiple voicings where applicable.

📈

Progress tracking

The app tracks your results per chord and per mode, so you can see where your recognition is solid and where it still needs work.

🆓

Free, no fuss

No subscription, no sign-in required to play, no ads. Just practice.

Chord tiers are informed by frequency data from thousands of ukulele play-along videos in the UkeAlong index. Full data at ukealong.com/statistics/.

Help us get it right

The app is nearly ready. Before it goes live on the Play Store, a small group of testers helps us catch anything we've missed. As a beta tester you'll:

  • Join the beta group using your Google Play account
  • Find the install link in the group's welcome message
  • Use the app as you normally would and share feedback in the group

⚠️ You'll need a Google account linked to Google Play to receive access.
